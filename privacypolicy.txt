Privacy Policy – Swiss Uni Login Helper

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Chrome extension Swiss Uni Login Helper processes and for what purpose.

1. General Information

The extension Swiss Uni Login Helper is designed solely to speed up the login process for Swiss universities using Switch edu-ID.
The extension operates entirely locally within the user’s browser.

2. What data is processed?

The extension stores only the following information:

the university or organization selected by the user

a setting indicating whether automatic forwarding (“auto-forward”) is enabled

This data is required to automate login steps and prevent users from repeatedly selecting their institution.

3. What data is not collected?

The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following data:

personally identifiable information (e.g. name, email address, postal address)

authentication data (e.g. passwords, login credentials, PINs)

health-related information

financial or payment information

personal communications (emails, messages, chats)

location data (e.g. IP address, GPS location)

browsing history or web activity logs

user interaction data (e.g. clicks, mouse movement, scrolling, keystrokes)

website content (e.g. text, images, videos, audio)

4. Use of permissions

Storage permission is used to save the selected organization and user preferences locally in the browser.

Access to specific websites is required to detect Switch edu-ID and university login pages and to automate organization selection and navigation.

The extension only runs on pages that are necessary for the authentication process.

5. Data transmission

No data is transmitted to third parties.
All stored information remains locally on the user’s device.

6. Tracking and analytics

The extension does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising services.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of the extension changes. The latest version will always be available in the Chrome Web Store listing.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the extension, please contact the developer using the contact information provided in the Chrome Web Store.
